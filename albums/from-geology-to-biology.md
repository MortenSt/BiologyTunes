## Description

From Geology to Biology follows life's four-billion-year story in the order it happened. It begins with proton gradients in deep-sea vents and self-copying RNA. It moves through the first metabolism, the invention of photosynthesis, the oxygen catastrophe and the ancient partnership that gave us mitochondria. It ends with multicellular bodies built from a shared genetic toolkit. Orchestral bookends frame a journey through rock, electronica, folk and metal. Every lyric is scientifically grounded, so you can learn the history of life by listening to it.

Short version: Four billion years of life, from rock to body plan, in ten songs. Science you can sing along to.
