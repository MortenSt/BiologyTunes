# The Living Clock: A Song of Degree Days – lyric video

Forensic entomology: how blowfly development, driven by accumulated degree-days, works as a clock for the post-mortem interval.

🎬 [`the-living-clock.mp4`](the-living-clock.mp4)

| Section | Animation |
|---|---|
| Intro | Night, wind and crickets; title |
| Verse 1 | Cell breakdown and a scent plume calling flies; species and habitats (Lucilia sericata, Calliphora vicina, C. vomitoria) |
| Chorus | Degree-day clock: temperature curve above a threshold fills an ADD counter that drives the hand from egg to adult |
| Verse 2 | Eggs, then 1st, 2nd and 3rd instar growing |
| Instrumental | Two weeks of day/night temperature with a cold spell that pauses development |
| Bridge | Prepupae leave and burrow into soil; Protophormia terraenovae pupates on site through frost and rain |
| Outro | Puparium opens, adult expands its wings and flies |

The carcass is a stylized deer lying on its side (non-graphic).

## Files
- `audio.mp3` – the song from Suno
- `lyrics.txt` – lyrics (input to `../tools/align_lyrics.py`)
- `timing.json`, `words.json` – timings (stable-ts on the vocal stem; held line endings moved by rule, see commit)
- `beats.json` – beat times
- `make_video.py` – draws the video
