# Secrets of the Female Body – lyric video

Female reproductive anatomy as clean, labelled textbook diagrams (schematic, not realistic).

🎬 [`secrets-of-the-female-body.mp4`](secrets-of-the-female-body.mp4)

| Section | Animation |
|---|---|
| Verse 1 | Vulva diagram, parts light up as they are sung; then the mostly internal clitoris structure |
| Pre-Chorus | Outside vs inside; pelvic floor; cervical screening and HPV vaccine |
| Chorus | Internal organs; light travels from vagina to ovaries; egg moves along the tube |
| Verse 2 | Vagina, cervix, uterus, fallopian tubes |
| Bridge | Ovary close-up with ovulation and estrogen; 28-day cycle chart with hormones and uterine lining |
| Verse 3 | Bartholin's glands; arousal and lubrication |
| Outro | Both diagrams and title |

Echo lines in parentheses are shown in pink after each line.

## Stem-driven animation
- **Drums/Percussion:** light particles and labels popping in the last chorus
- **Bass:** pulse in the highlighted organ and the pelvic floor
- **Keyboard:** nerve signals along the clitoris
- **Strings:** background light; **Brass:** chorus glow; **Backing Vocals:** echo text

## Files
