Cellular respiration, told in the order it evolved, and sung by ATP synthase, the rotary enzyme so ancient it traces back to LUCA, the last universal common ancestor. Every breath you take runs these pathways in the same sequence they appeared over four billion years.

🧬 WHAT THE SONG COVERS
• The vent: alkaline hydrothermal vents held a natural proton gradient across thin iron-sulfur mineral walls. Before any enzymes existed, minerals acted as the first catalysts.
• The ancient core: glycolysis splits glucose into two pyruvate and nets 2 ATP without any oxygen.
• The wheel that ran backwards: the Krebs cycle first ran in reverse, fixing CO₂ to build the body, before it turned forward to burn fuel.
• The poison that became power: cyanobacteria filled the air with oxygen about 2.4 billion years ago. Oxygen became the final electron acceptor (roughly 10× more ATP), and an engulfed bacterium became the mitochondrion.
